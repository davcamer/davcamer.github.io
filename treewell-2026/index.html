Treewell 2026

This is a documentation of seeds planted in the treewell in front of the building in spring 2026. Three seed mixes from Hudson Valley Seed Company were combined and broadcast into the treewell, with the goal of creating a dense, wildlife-friendly planting of natives and companion annuals that can sustain itself season to season.

The Seed Mixes

  • Bird Lover’s Wildflower Seed Mix — A blend of annuals, biennials, and perennials selected to produce abundant seed for birds, with bright, long-blooming flowers that also attract pollinators.
  • Eastern Pollinator Mix — A mix focused on native and naturalized plants that provide nectar and pollen for bees, butterflies, and other insects across the growing season.
  • Northeast Native Mix — A curated selection of wildflowers native to the northeastern United States, emphasizing long-lived perennials with strong ecological value.

All three mixes were sown together in spring 2026.
