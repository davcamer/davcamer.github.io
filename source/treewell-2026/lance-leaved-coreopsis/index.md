*Coreopsis lanceolata*

**Seed Mixes:** Bird Lover's, Eastern Pollinator

---

## About

Lance-Leaved Coreopsis is a cheerful native perennial found in open, rocky or sandy areas of central and eastern North America. It produces bright golden-yellow daisy-like flowers on long, slender stems above a low mound of narrow, lance-shaped leaves — the namesake that distinguishes it from other coreopsis species. Growing 1–2 feet tall, it is one of the longest-blooming native perennials available, flowering from late spring into late summer, especially if spent flowers are regularly removed. It establishes readily and is highly adaptable to a range of conditions.

## Wildlife Value

Lance-Leaved Coreopsis attracts bees, butterflies, and beetles to its flowers. The seeds are eaten by birds. Particularly notable are the specialist Coreopsis-mining bees (*Calliopsis* species), which depend on coreopsis pollen as a food source for their larvae and are found wherever coreopsis blooms. Supporting specialist bees like these is one of the most targeted ecological benefits a garden can provide.

## In the Treewell

Lance-Leaved Coreopsis is a genuine workhorse of the wildflower garden — easy to grow, long-lived, and consistently floriferous. It tolerates poor, dry soils well and will spread slowly by seed over time. Deadheading the spent flowers will extend the bloom season significantly. A plant that rewards a little attention but doesn't demand it.

## Growing Notes

- **Type:** Perennial
- **Height:** 1–2 ft
- **Bloom time:** May–August
- **Sun needs:** Full sun
- **Water needs:** Low to moderate; tolerates dry, lean soils

## Seedling Identification

Cotyledons are oval to slightly oblong. The first true leaves are narrow and lance-shaped — noticeably elongated relative to their width — opposite, and smooth to very slightly hairy. The opposite arrangement of narrow, elongated, bright green leaves is the key feature. Plains Coreopsis seedlings (also in the mix) look similar but have more deeply divided leaves at an early stage; Lance-Leaved Coreopsis leaves remain simple and undivided.
