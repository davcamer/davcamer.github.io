This is a documentation of seeds planted in the treewell in front of the building in spring 2026. Three seed mixes from [Hudson Valley Seed Company](https://hudsonvalleyseed.com/) were combined and broadcast into the treewell, with the goal of creating a dense, wildlife-friendly planting of natives and companion annuals that can sustain itself season to season.

## The Seed Mixes

- **[Bird Lover's Wildflower Seed Mix](https://hudsonvalleyseed.com/products/bird-lovers-mix)** — A blend of annuals, biennials, and perennials selected to produce abundant seed for birds, with bright, long-blooming flowers that also attract pollinators.
- **[Eastern Pollinator Mix](https://hudsonvalleyseed.com/products/eastern-pollinator-mix)** — A mix focused on native and naturalized plants that provide nectar and pollen for bees, butterflies, and other insects across the growing season.
- **[Northeast Native Mix](https://hudsonvalleyseed.com/products/north-east-native-mix)** — A curated selection of wildflowers native to the northeastern United States, emphasizing long-lived perennials with strong ecological value.

All three mixes were sown together in spring 2026.
